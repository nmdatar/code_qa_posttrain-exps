# Preregistered training reward alignment: aligned-coverage-v1

Prepared 2026-09-29 before launching expanded experiments. This document freezes a candidate design; it does not claim a completed experiment or calibrated judge.

## Intervention and controls

Compare running-baseline REINFORCE with `positive-coverage-v4` against the same algorithm with `aligned-coverage-v1`. Keep base checkpoint, seeds, task membership/order, rollout count, optimizer settings, v6 rubrics, v7 full semantic grader, evidence policy, harness, stopping rule and strict evaluation fixed. The larger campaign specifies cohort size and seed schedule. Prefer three paired seeds and at least 128 distinct training tasks per seed; use the largest common cohort that the approved worst-case budget supports. No early success declaration based on reward curves. Do not change coefficients after observing selection results.

The coverage base is exactly the same `independent-factual-coverage-v3` pass as the control, supplied through the `factual_coverage` argument; semantic findings supply penalties and eligibility. The feedback definition records the coverage version and penalty weights. The frozen training scalar is `clip(supported_required_coverage - .75*contradictions - .15*unsupported_extras - .05*uncited_assertions - .05*bad_citations, 0, 1)`. Supported required facts receive rubric-weighted full or half credit. Other facts earn no positive credit. The coefficients come from the repository's existing `supported-coverage-v3`, not a fit to held-out answers. Unsupported assertions are different from contradictions: missing evidence alone never incurs the contradiction penalty. Count each additional claim once within a category; source-established required/global defects get one fallback contradiction penalty if none is already counted among additional assertions. Unsupported citation IDs are deduplicated. Deterministic invalid-citation reasons impose at least one bad-citation penalty; they are not used as source evidence. False execution claims earn zero only when accompanied by a source-linked negative finding. Non-answer modes earn zero. This is a clipped additive reward; substantial errors can erase partial credit.

Every contradiction/material finding must identify nonempty source evidence already bound by the full grader. Global critical, diagram or false-execution flags without an associated source-linked negative finding are excluded. Review flags, disagreements, incomplete assessments, and unresolved strict grades are excluded rather than scored zero. Source-reference checks are a minimum evidence requirement, not independent confirmation of the judge's correctness. Independent source review of a training-only negative sample remains useful before spending on a large run.

Strict evaluation is unchanged and retains its existing decision rules. `tier-quality-v1`, `tier-efficiency-v1`, and existing harness experiments remain untouched: acceptance-gating and compute penalties are separate interventions.

## Validation completed locally

` .venv-eval/bin/python -m unittest tests.test_reward_alignment -q ` passes 36 tests, including inherited full-grader regression cases. New controls use a local source-backed synthetic training fixture and cover correct/partial/incorrect ranking, source-backed falsehoods, missing or invented evidence, unsupported-vs-contradicted findings, filler dilution, citation penalties, global flags without linked evidence, required contradictions without material flags, immutable inputs, malformed fixed weights and duplicate claim IDs. Tests also verify exact independent-coverage substitution, unresolved/invalid coverage handling and preservation of strict eligibility. The integration fixture runs v7 extraction and assessment and verifies strict score remains zero while training feedback receives partial factual credit. Mock judge fixtures test software behavior; they do not measure live judge accuracy.

A diagnostic replay used only the 128 `split=train` trajectories from archived `reinforce-v6-seed42-v1`; selection/confirmation answers were not used to choose or modify coefficients. The frozen v6 reference contains equal-weight claims, and replay rebuilt IDs/weights from those fixed training references. Replay supplied each archived independent factual-coverage score as the reward base. Of 128 trajectories, 98 had eligible complete assessments, 24 received positive aligned reward, and mean reward among eligible trajectories was 0.1663265306122449 (control factual-coverage mean on those same 98 trajectories: 0.2792517006802721). Another 24 had no full semantic assessment, two had unresolved assessments, and four contained negative findings without linked source evidence. No new judge calls or paid inference were made. This is a feasibility projection, not evidence that training improves performance.

## Integration and interpretation

Integration is complete in config validation and training collection; v7 is required. Training collection retains supported factual scoring for bad citations by removing invalid references from judge evidence and passing deterministic citation errors into `feedback`. Run the same independent factual-coverage pass as the control and pass `coverage_assessment.score` into `feedback(factual_coverage=...)`; also require full semantic findings for penalties. Never reconstruct penalties from coverage-only output. Explicitly missing factual coverage is excluded, and invalid numerical coverage is rejected. A feedback result with `eligible=false` must become unresolved verification with `reward=None`, not a resolved sample. Preserve strict result and semantic findings in telemetry.

The existing positive-coverage branch can recover training rewards with an independent factual-coverage grader when strict assessment fails. Alignment cannot safely reconstruct penalties without full semantic findings. The proposed integration runs both grading passes in both arms and retains the same factual base; penalty eligibility can still differ because alignment requires a full assessment. Thus effective coverage and exclusions can differ even with identical grading calls. Report this mechanism explicitly; compare attempted trajectories, eligible trajectories, updates and grading calls. Do not claim this isolates only penalty coefficients if fallback eligibility differs.

The primary comparison uses paired held-out strict-credit changes at fixed terminal checkpoints, with strict passes and resolved coverage alongside them. Report effects per seed and pooled task-paired uncertainty; selection scores are model-selection evidence, not untouched final confirmation. Reward variance, zero-reward fraction, contradiction/unsupported/citation component counts, and source-link exclusions are secondary diagnostics. Same learning rate does not ensure equal effective update scale after changing reward. Keep confirmation untouched until the prespecified model-selection procedure chooses a final candidate.

Integration regression command `.venv-eval/bin/python -m unittest tests.test_reward_alignment tests.test_shaped_reward tests.test_coverage_judge tests.test_collection_training tests.test_experiment_config -q` passes 106 tests. This covers unchanged strict development routing, exact independent coverage base, invalid-reference evidence filtering, unresolved exclusion, v7-only validation, and coefficient-sensitive alignment identity without changing positive-control identity.
