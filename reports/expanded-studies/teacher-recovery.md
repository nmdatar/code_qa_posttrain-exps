# Teacher collection recovery after provider-credit interruption

The stopped controller was fully archived (7,757 files, no JSON parse errors; exit 137 from the explicit termination). All recovery source files are checked against `billing-stop-archive.json` before freezing the new task manifest.

The original collection requested 1,200 attempt slots on 600 training tasks. The archive contains 1,058 trajectories across 529 tasks. **955 existing attempt outcomes are preserved**, regardless of reward or strict pass/fail. This includes 16 completed answers with unresolved grades and two infrastructure-labelled trajectories that already contain a final answer. Their original answer generations will not be sampled again; any grading repair must reuse their frozen evidence separately.

The recovery manifest requests exactly **245 new episodes**: 103 infrastructure failures without a final answer, and 142 missing attempt slots. Partial tool-only failed traces remain in provenance; resuming their former sandbox is not safe after controller termination, so only those failed slots restart. The collection identity, original task membership, sampling temperature, model, grader, limits and reward remain fixed. Automatic group retries are disabled. Each new trajectory group ID records its original synthetic attempt slot. Historical random episode IDs do not contain launch ordinals; their deterministic sorted-ID mapping is explicitly described as synthetic.

Prepared config: `configs/experiments/expanded-studies-recovery-v1/teacher-recovery.json`. The recovery route is permitted only for a sampling-only benchmark, attempts=1, retries=0. Its hash-pinned manifest partitions all original 1,200 slots into preserved and replacement slots, checks source provenance and rejects attempts to replace semantic failures. It uses the original authoritative teacher ledger `artifacts/project-budget/expanded-studies-v1/teacher.json` and its $2,200 cap. The additional conservative reservation bound is **$416.759676**, including a full 24-hour controller allowance; no new cap or provider invoice is implied.

The complete-investigation SFT audit on the partial collection found 151 strict-passing trajectories, but only 40 met the existing clean tool/source/native-token admission contract. These collapse to **31 lineages across 13 families**, short of the unchanged 200-lineage/15-family gate. The 405 perfect factual-reward trajectories are not 405 verified demonstrations. The failed gate is saved in `data/sft/complete-investigations-teacher-partial-v1/audit.json`; completing recovery does not guarantee the gate will pass. No tiny substitute SFT run is authorized by this audit.

Validation: eight new recovery tests plus the existing remote/research tests passed (23 total). Tests cover preserved zero-reward/unresolved answers, infrastructure retry boundaries, missing slot multiplicities, immutable archive completeness/hashes, attempted replacement of preserved outcomes, scientific-condition changes, benchmark-only enforcement and persisted slot IDs. No paid calls were made in recovery preparation.
